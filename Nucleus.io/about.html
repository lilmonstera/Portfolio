Ownership Model

Built to own what it builds

Nucleus isn't a systems integrator reselling someone else's technology.

The larger of the two is a technology deployment and advisory practice — the engine that put Nucleus infrastructure inside Colgate's global surveillance network, Woolworths' national footprint, FIFA World Cup stadia, and Sasol's critical operations.
