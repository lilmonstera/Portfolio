Featured IP: 01

MTLS — the Modular Traffic Light System

Developed in direct response to the toll rigid steel traffic infrastructure takes in road accidents, MTLS replaces it with lightweight, modular components engineered to reduce harm on impact.

download Download MTLS Brochure

Impact Case Study

Installed at one of Johannesburg's highest-collision intersections, the system ran seven years with a single accident recorded — a result that speaks for itself.

Technical Specification

Built into the system is number-plate recognition, streaming live over fibre to security control rooms: infrastructure that protects lives and doubles as a security asset.

Award Winning

MTLS has been recognised with a SABS Design Award for Excellence, featured in a national retrospective on South African design, displayed at the Smithsonian in New York, and named the only African winner of the China Red Star Design Award — one of the largest design honours in the world.

Featured IP: 02

Meerkat — cable theft prevention

An underground system built to stop cable theft at the point of extraction rather than after the fact. One unit physically prevents low- and medium-voltage cable from being pulled from the ground; a second protects high-value, high-voltage cable with early-warning GPS technology that alerts a response team the moment a theft begins. Recognised as a winner of the Jozi My Beginning programme.
