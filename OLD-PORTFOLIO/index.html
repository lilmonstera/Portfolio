Who I am

I taught myself to code, then went and did it for real — shipping two live websites for a Webflow agency, learning Python and C through Harvard's CS50, and now deep in a full-stack bootcamp because one language was never going to be enough. I'm not the finished product. I'm the developer who shows up, asks the right questions, and gets better every single week.

What I bring

Real client work, not just tutorials. At PurpleGlobe, I designed and built two professional websites from the ground up — CMS setup, copywriting, imagery, layout, the lot — for a fast-paced, deadline-driven team that trusted me with their front end.

Credibility that isn't just self-reported: my former manager at PurpleGlobe called my work diligent and detail-oriented, with a solid grasp of both the design and development sides of front-end work. My support coach at UCT Online High School described me as proactive, punctual, and unafraid to ask for help when I need it — which, in this industry, is a feature, not a bug.
