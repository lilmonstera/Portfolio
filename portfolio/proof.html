On the Record
This is the proof — what she trained in, what she taught herself, and what she's still building on the side.

It starts at the UCT OHS, which she still calls the defining achievement of her creative life — three years of being trained to treat design as a discipline rather than a talent you either have or don't. Everything since has been an argument for that same idea: that good work is built, not born.

She's self-taught on the code side — HTML, CSS, and JavaScript learned by shipping real sites rather than following a syllabus — and is now formalizing that with a Year 1 Computer Science certification and a Website Design short course through Red & Yellow. In between, there's a novel she's plotting chapter by chapter, because the instinct to structure a story and the instinct to structure a client brief turned out to be the same instinct.
